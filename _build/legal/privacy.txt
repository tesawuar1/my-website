Komodo & Ko. ("we", "our" or "us") is a travel concierge and trip coordination business
specializing in travel experiences in Labuan Bajo, Komodo National Park and surrounding

We coordinate travel arrangements and bookings through a network of independent service
providers, including yacht and boat operators, hotels, transportation companies, tour guides and
other local partners.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, Komodo & Ko. is responsible for handling the personal
information collected through its website, communication channels and booking services,
subject to applicable data protection laws.

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us using the details provided in
Section 12.
