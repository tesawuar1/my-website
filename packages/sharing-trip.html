Sharing Trip

3 days, 2 nights

Home/Package/Sharing Trip

Join other travellers on a yacht for 3 days and 2 nights. It is a great-value way to see the best of Komodo, with your own cabin and the crew taking care of everything.

Sample itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Labuan Bajo pick-up, Kelor Island, snorkeling at Manjarite and sunset at Kalong Island.

  2. Day 2

    Padar Island sunrise, Pink Beach, Komodo Island trek, Taka Makassar and Manta Point.

  3. Day 3

    Turtles at Siaba Island, Kanawa Island, then back to Labuan Bajo in the afternoon.

This is a sample route. We adjust the final plan to the weather, tides, season and what you would like to see.

Typically includes

  • Boat with captain and crew
  • Meals on board, drinking water, coffee and tea
  • Snorkeling equipment
  • Pick-up and drop-off in Labuan Bajo
  • Trip coordination before and during your journey

The exact inclusions are confirmed in your personal quote.
