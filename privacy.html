Komodo & Ko. | Last updated: 17 November 2026

At Komodo & Ko., we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share your personal information when you visit our website, contact our team, make an enquiry or book travel experiences and related services through us.

By using our website or engaging with our services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Who We Are

Komodo & Ko. ("we", "our" or "us") is a travel concierge and trip coordination business specializing in travel experiences in Labuan Bajo, Komodo National Park and surrounding destinations.

We coordinate travel arrangements and bookings through a network of independent service providers, including yacht and boat operators, hotels, transportation companies, tour guides and other local partners.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, Komodo & Ko. is responsible for handling the personal information collected through its website, communication channels and booking services, subject to applicable data protection laws.

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us using the details provided in Section 12.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect different categories of personal information depending on how you interact with our website and services.

Identity and Contact Information

This may include:

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone or WhatsApp number.
  • Country of residence.
  • Nationality.
  • Passport information, where required for travel registration, vessel manifests, permits or other official requirements.

Travel and Booking Information

To arrange and coordinate your travel experience, we may collect:

  • Intended travel dates.
  • Number of passengers.
  • Selected vessel, yacht or boat.
  • Cabin preferences.
  • Travel itinerary and destination preferences.
  • Flight and accommodation details.
  • Dietary requirements and special requests.
  • Diving or snorkeling experience, where relevant.
  • Other information necessary to organize your trip.

Communications and Enquiries

We may retain communications exchanged through:

  • Website contact forms.
  • Email.
  • WhatsApp.
  • Telephone calls.
  • Instagram and other social media platforms.
  • Other communication channels used by Komodo & Ko.

This includes enquiries, booking discussions, service requests and our responses.

Payment and Billing Information

When processing bookings, we may collect payment-related information such as:

  • Transaction references.
  • Payment confirmations.
  • Billing details.
  • Bank transfer information.
  • Other information necessary to verify and reconcile payments.

Payments may be processed through banks or third-party payment providers. We do not intentionally store complete payment card details on our website servers.

Technical and Website Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected, including:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device information.
  • Pages visited.
  • Website interaction data.
  • Referral sources.
  • Cookie and similar technology information, where applicable.

3. How We Use Your Information

Komodo & Ko. uses personal information only for legitimate business and service-related purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and providing travel recommendations.
  • Preparing quotations and customized travel arrangements.
  • Coordinating yacht charters, boat trips, hotel stays, transportation and other travel experiences.
  • Processing and managing reservations.
  • Communicating booking confirmations, invoices and payment instructions.
  • Providing itineraries, travel information and pre-departure guidance.
  • Sharing relevant guest information with service providers responsible for delivering the booked services.
  • Verifying payments and preventing fraudulent transactions.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, tax and government requirements.
  • Improving our website, services and overall guest experience.
  • Sending promotional information and service updates where permitted by applicable law.

We process personal information based on the applicable legal grounds, which may include your consent, the performance of a booking or service agreement, compliance with legal obligations, and our legitimate business interests.

4. WhatsApp and Other Communication Channels

Komodo & Ko. uses WhatsApp and other communication channels to communicate with prospective and existing guests. When you contact us through WhatsApp, your messages and related information are processed through the WhatsApp service operated by Meta Platforms. WhatsApp may process your information in accordance with its own privacy policies and terms of service.

We may use these communications to:

  • Respond to enquiries and travel-related questions.
  • Discuss travel preferences and booking requirements.
  • Share quotations, invoices and booking documents.
  • Provide trip updates and pre-departure information.
  • Maintain records of customer communications for booking management and customer service purposes.

If we use third-party messaging or customer relationship management (CRM) platforms to manage communications, relevant information may also be processed by those providers on our behalf.

5. How We Share Your Information

Komodo & Ko. does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

However, certain information may need to be shared with trusted parties when necessary to arrange and deliver your requested services.

These parties may include:

Our Internal Team

Authorized personnel responsible for managing enquiries, reservations, customer service and travel coordination.

Travel and Service Providers

Independent suppliers involved in delivering your travel arrangements, including:

  • Yacht and boat operators.
  • Vessel crew members.
  • Hotels and resorts.
  • Villa accommodation providers.
  • Transportation companies and drivers.
  • Tour guides and activity providers.
  • Diving and snorkeling operators.
  • Other relevant local service partners.

Technology and Communication Providers Third-party platforms that support our website, communication systems, booking management and payment processing.

Financial Institutions and Payment Providers

Banks and payment service providers involved in processing or verifying transactions.

Government Authorities

Relevant government agencies, immigration authorities, port authorities, national park authorities or other official institutions when disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

We only share information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

6. Cookies and Advertising Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand visitor behavior and measure marketing performance.

Depending on the technologies implemented on our website, these may include:

  • Essential Cookies: Support basic website functionality and security.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember user preferences and browsing settings.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand website traffic and visitor interactions.
  • Advertising Technologies: Help measure advertising performance and, where applicable, deliver relevant advertisements through platforms such as Google, Meta, Facebook and Instagram.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or browsing experience of some parts of our website.

Where required by applicable law, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies or advertising technologies.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or to meet applicable legal requirements.

Our retention practices may include:

Booking and Travel Records Reservation forms, invoices, payment confirmations and travel documentation may be retained for accounting, operational, legal and administrative purposes.

Customer Communications

Messages, enquiries and customer service records may be retained to manage existing bookings, respond to follow-up requests and maintain service records.

Marketing Information

Marketing-related information may be retained until you withdraw your consent or request to stop receiving promotional communications, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Technical Information

Website analytics and technical records may be retained for limited periods to monitor website performance, improve security and support operational requirements.

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and the data protection laws applicable to you, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

These rights may include:

  • Right to Access: Request information about the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent where your information is processed based on consent.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request a copy of your personal information in a commonly used, structured and machine-readable format, where applicable.
  • Right to Object: Object to certain types of personal data processing where permitted by applicable law.
  • Right to Request Deletion: Request the deletion of personal information where legally applicable.

To exercise your privacy rights, please contact us using the contact details provided in this policy. We may request additional information to verify your identity before processing your request.

If you believe your personal information has been handled improperly, you may also have the right to submit a complaint to the relevant data protection authority in your jurisdiction.

9. International Data Transfers

Because Komodo & Ko. works with travel suppliers, technology providers and service partners, certain personal information may be transferred or accessed outside your country of residence.

For example, information may need to be shared with accommodation providers, vessel operators, booking platforms or other service providers involved in arranging your journey.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that such transfers are handled in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website or communications may contain links to third-party websites, booking platforms or external services.

These external websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies and data handling practices.

Komodo & Ko. does not control the privacy practices or security procedures of third-party websites.

We encourage guests to review the relevant privacy policies before submitting personal information through external platforms.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Komodo & Ko. may revise or update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business operations, services, technology or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page together with the revised "Last Updated" date.

Where appropriate, we may also notify guests of significant changes through email or other communication channels.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to stay informed about how their personal information is handled.
