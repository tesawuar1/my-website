Siaba Island

Snorkel with sea turtles

What Is Siaba Island

Siaba is a small island with a sheltered bay known for its sea turtles. The calm, shallow water and seagrass make it one of the most reliable places in the park to snorkel with green turtles.

Where Is Siaba Island

Siaba lies inside Komodo National Park, about 2 hours from Labuan Bajo by boat, and fits easily into a day or multi-day route.

Why Siaba Island Is a Must-Visit

Seeing turtles graze peacefully a few metres below you is unforgettable, and the gentle conditions suit almost everyone.

What to Expect in Siaba Island

Calm conditions most of the time, so it suits beginners and families. Please keep a respectful distance and never touch the turtles.

Best Time to Visit Siaba Island

The dry season, from about April to November, brings calm seas, clear water and sunny days. Mornings are usually calmest.

Things to Do in Siaba Island

  • Snorkel with green turtles
  • Swim in the sheltered bay
  • Take underwater photos
  • Relax on deck between swims
