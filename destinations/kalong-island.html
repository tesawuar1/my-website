Kalong Island

Thousands of flying foxes at sunset

What Is Kalong Island

Kalong means "flying fox" in Indonesian. This small mangrove island is home to a large colony of fruit bats, and every evening at sunset thousands of them take to the sky.

Where Is Kalong Island

Kalong lies near Rinca Island, in the east of the national park, about 2 hours from Labuan Bajo by boat. Many overnight boats anchor nearby to watch the show.

Why Kalong Island Is a Must-Visit

Watching the sky fill with bats against a golden sunset is one of the most memorable natural sights in Komodo.

What to Expect in Kalong Island

You watch from the boat; there is no landing on the island. The bats start leaving at dusk and stream overhead toward the larger islands to feed, usually for 20 to 30 minutes.

Best Time to Visit Kalong Island

At sunset, any time of year. The dry season, from about April to November, offers the calmest anchorages.

Things to Do in Kalong Island

  • Watch the flying foxes take flight
  • Enjoy the sunset from the deck
  • Have dinner on deck under the stars
  • Take silhouette photos at dusk
