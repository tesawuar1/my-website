Kanawa Island

A peaceful island with an easy reef

What Is Kanawa Island

Kanawa is a small, peaceful island with a long wooden jetty, white sand and one of the easiest house reefs in the area. It is a favourite place to slow down after the busier sights.

Where Is Kanawa Island

Kanawa sits north-west of Labuan Bajo, around 1 to 1.5 hours by boat, on the edge of Komodo National Park.

Why Kanawa Island Is a Must-Visit

You can snorkel straight from the jetty, often with turtles, starfish and plenty of reef fish. There is also a small island resort, so you can stay for lunch, sunset or the night.

What to Expect in Kanawa Island

Clear, shallow water right off the jetty with coral, blue starfish and frequent turtles. A relaxed atmosphere with a restaurant on the island.

Best Time to Visit Kanawa Island

The dry season, from about April to November, brings calm seas, clear water and sunny days. Mornings usually have the clearest water.

Things to Do in Kanawa Island

  • Snorkel from the jetty
  • Swim with sea turtles
  • Walk up for a sunset view
  • Stay overnight at the island resort
