Sebayur Island

Calm water and coral gardens

What Is Sebayur Island

Sebayur is a small, quiet island with soft white sand and calm turquoise water. It is popular for easy snorkeling and relaxing between bigger sights, and a gentle hill offers views over the nearby islands.

Where Is Sebayur Island

Sebayur sits close to Labuan Bajo, on the edge of Komodo National Park, roughly 1 to 1.5 hours away by boat. It is often included on day trips or as a calm stop on the way back to port.

Why Sebayur Island Is a Must-Visit

It is a relaxed, uncrowded place with colourful coral close to the shore, which makes it ideal for beginners, families and anyone who wants a slower moment in the day.

What to Expect in Sebayur Island

Shallow, easy entry from the beach, colourful coral and plenty of reef fish. Facilities are limited, which keeps the island peaceful.

Best Time to Visit Sebayur Island

The dry season, from about April to November, brings calm seas, clear water and sunny days. Mornings usually have the calmest water.

Things to Do in Sebayur Island

  • Snorkel the coral garden off the beach
  • Walk up the hill for a view of the islands
  • Swim and relax on the white sand
  • Enjoy lunch on board in the sheltered bay
