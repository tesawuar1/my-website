Pink Beach

Soft pink sand and coral

What Is Pink Beach

Pink Beach is one of only a handful of pink-sand beaches in the world. Its soft blush colour comes from tiny fragments of red organisms that live on the coral reef, mixed with white sand.

Where Is Pink Beach

Pink Beach is on Komodo Island, inside Komodo National Park. It is usually visited on the same day as the Komodo dragon trek, around 3 to 4 hours from Labuan Bajo by phinisi or about 2 hours by speedboat.

Why Pink Beach Is a Must-Visit

The colour is at its best in bright sunlight and in the shallows, and the reef starts just a few metres from the shore. It is a rare chance to swim, snorkel and relax on a beach that looks like nowhere else.

What to Expect in Pink Beach

A quiet, curved beach with clear, shallow water and healthy coral close in. There are no shops or facilities, so bring water, sun protection and reef-safe sunscreen. The pink tone is subtle in places and strongest where the waves wash over the sand.

Best Time to Visit Pink Beach

Visit in the dry season, from about April to November, for calm water and good visibility. Late morning to midday, when the sun is high, brings out the pink colour best.

Things to Do in Pink Beach

  • Snorkel the reef just off the beach
  • Swim in calm, clear water
  • Take photos of the pink shoreline from the boat
  • Relax on the sand between island visits
