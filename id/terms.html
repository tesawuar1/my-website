1. How Your Trip Document Works

At Komodo & Ko., we believe a well-organized journey begins with clear communication and transparent arrangements.

Before confirming your trip, our reservation team will provide you with the reservation form and applicable Terms & Conditions through our official communication channels.

We encourage every guest to review the documents carefully before proceeding with payment.

Booking and signing procedure:

  1. Our team confirms the availability of your selected trip.
  2. You receive the reservation form and applicable Terms & Conditions.
  3. Review the information and ask our team if you need clarification.
  4. Complete the reservation form and sign the Terms & Conditions digitally.
  5. Return the signed documents through our official communication channel.
  6. We issue your invoice and payment instructions after receiving the completed documents.
  7. Your booking is confirmed once the required payment has been received and verified.

For group reservations, one designated representative may sign on behalf of the group, provided all participants have reviewed and agreed to the applicable conditions.

By signing the trip agreement or completing the booking process, guests acknowledge that they have read, understood and accepted the applicable Terms & Conditions.

2. Guest Acknowledgment & Signature

3. Private Trip — Terms & Conditions

3.1. What's Included

Your Private Trip package includes:

  • Accommodation onboard the selected vessel throughout the journey.
  • Photo and video documentation using drone, GoPro and mirrorless cameras (raw footage only).
  • Daily meals during the trip.
  • Mineral water, coffee and tea.
  • Professional tour guide.
  • Snorkeling equipment.
  • Life jackets.
  • Pick-up and drop-off between your hotel or airport and the designated meeting point, exclusively within the Labuan Bajo mainland area near the harbor.

Transportation from accommodations located on other islands or in the Manjaga area is not included.

3.2. What's Not Included

The following expenses are not covered by the package:

  • Crew gratuities.
  • Domestic and international flight tickets.
  • Alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages.
  • Personal snacks.
  • Komodo National Park entrance and activity fees, subject to applicable government regulations.
  • Optional drone permit for Komodo National Park.

The estimated drone permit fee is approximately USD 130 per drone, subject to change. The permit is optional and must be requested at least seven days before departure.

3.3. Recommended Items to Bring

To make your journey more comfortable, we recommend bringing:

  • Sunscreen.
  • Sunglasses.
  • Beach hat.
  • Personal snacks and supplements.
  • Extra clothing.
  • Toiletries.
  • Swimwear.
  • Wet wipes.
  • Power bank.
  • Dry bag.
  • Personal medication.
  • Telkomsel SIM card.
  • Hiking sandals.
  • Additional cash.

3.4. Important Travel Information

Flight arrangements

Flight schedules and direct routes to Labuan Bajo may vary depending on the airline and travel season. Guests are advised to check flight availability through the airline's official booking channels.

Flights to Labuan Bajo are available through routes connecting Jakarta (CGK), Surabaya (SUB) and Bali (DPS), subject to airline schedules.

Our team can assist with general flight and accommodation recommendations upon request.

Arrival recommendation

We strongly recommend arriving in Labuan Bajo at least one day before your scheduled departure. Guests arriving on the departure date must be in Labuan Bajo and ready by 9:00 AM WITA (GMT+8).

Komodo & Ko. cannot be held responsible for disruptions resulting from airline delays, cancellations or missed connections.

During the trip

Guests are encouraged to communicate any concerns or service-related issues directly to the onboard crew or assigned tour guide as soon as they arise.

This allows the team to assess the situation and provide an appropriate solution while the trip is still in progress.

High-season conditions

During peak travel periods, popular destinations such as Padar Island, Pink Beach, Komodo Island and Manta Point may experience higher visitor numbers.

Crowded conditions at these destinations may be beyond the control of the trip organizer.

Service complaints

Any concerns regarding onboard services should be reported immediately to the Tour Leader or Cruise Director.

Complaints submitted after the conclusion of the trip may not be eligible for further consideration.

All reservations are subject to vessel availability and must be confirmed before payment.

  • Full name.

  • The deposit is non-refundable once the booking is confirmed.
  • The remaining balance must be settled no later than 30 days before departure.
  • For bookings made less than 30 days before departure, full payment is required.

Payment confirmation

Guests must send their payment receipt through the official Komodo & Ko. communication channel for verification.

Photography and video documentation

During the trip, photo and video documentation may be provided as part of the package.

Guests should review the applicable photography and media consent arrangements before departure. Any promotional use of identifiable guest footage must be subject to the relevant consent.

3.6. Payment & Billing Policy

Pricing

Published rates are applicable to the specified travel period and may be adjusted annually or when operational costs change.

Price adjustments may result from changes in fuel costs, additional routes, activities, government regulations or other operational circumstances.

The confirmed invoice will specify the applicable price and payment amount.

Advance reservations

For bookings made approximately one year in advance, the following payment schedule applies:

  • 30% initial deposit to secure the reservation.
  • Additional 20% payment, due 120 days before departure.
  • Remaining balance, due 30 days before departure.

Specific vessel arrangements may be subject to additional operator conditions.

Child policy

Children aged three years and above are charged the full applicable rate.

Bank charges

All transaction and bank transfer fees must be covered by the sender.

The amount received must match the amount stated on the invoice. Any shortfall caused by transfer fees or intermediary bank charges remains the responsibility of the guest.

3.7. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Guest-initiated cancellation

Once a reservation has been confirmed and payment received, cancellation by the guest is subject to the following conditions:

  • Deposits are non-refundable.
  • Payments made towards the reservation are non-refundable.
  • Cancellations do not qualify for refunds, rescheduling or transfer to another trip.
  • Guests may nominate another person to take their place, subject to approval and applicable registration requirements.

Force majeure and unforeseen circumstances

Force majeure refers to circumstances beyond reasonable human control that may prevent the trip from operating as originally scheduled.

Examples include:

  • Natural disasters.
  • Severe weather conditions.
  • Civil unrest.
  • Disease outbreaks.
  • Government restrictions.
  • Official closures of destinations.
  • Other unforeseen circumstances affecting safe operations.

If such circumstances affect the scheduled journey, the itinerary or departure arrangements may be modified, postponed or cancelled in accordance with the applicable vessel operator's conditions.

Where an official government closure prevents the trip from operating, alternative scheduling will be coordinated subject to availability and the applicable operator agreement. Such circumstances do not automatically entitle guests to a cash refund.

Late arrivals

Late arrivals may require adjustments to the itinerary based on the remaining available time.

The vessel is not obligated to extend the original trip duration or accommodate additional hours resulting from a guest's late arrival.

Liability

Komodo & Ko. is not responsible for disruptions caused by:

  • Airline schedule changes or flight cancellations.
  • Lost, delayed or damaged baggage handled by airlines, hotels or other transportation providers.
  • Loss or damage to personal belongings during the journey.
  • Itinerary adjustments resulting from adverse weather, natural disasters or government restrictions.

Responsibilities and liabilities relating to vessel operations are also subject to the applicable service provider's agreement.

Guest responsibilities

All guests are responsible for:

  • Taking proper care of onboard facilities and equipment.
  • Following instructions from the Tour Leader and crew.
  • Maintaining appropriate conduct throughout the journey.
  • Complying with safety procedures and operational requirements.

Lost or damaged equipment, including life jackets, snorkeling masks and other facilities, may be charged at the applicable replacement cost.

Guests must not independently alter the agreed itinerary or undertake activities outside the approved arrangements without authorization from the vessel management.

Prohibited conduct

Illegal drug use, disruptive behavior or conduct that threatens the safety or comfort of other guests and crew members is strictly prohibited.

Guests who engage in such conduct may be removed from the trip without entitlement to a refund.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much deposit is required to book a trip?

A 50% deposit is generally required to secure your reservation. The remaining balance must be paid no later than 30 days before departure.

2. Can I cancel my reservation and receive a refund?

Deposits and payments are non-refundable once the reservation has been confirmed. Please review the applicable cancellation policy before proceeding.

3. What happens if the weather is bad?

Safety always comes first. Weather and sea conditions may require changes to the itinerary, route or scheduled activities. Our team will coordinate with the vessel operator regarding alternative arrangements.

4. Are Komodo National Park entrance fees included?

Unless specifically stated in your booking confirmation, National Park entrance fees and applicable government charges are paid separately.

5. Is there a minimum number of guests for an Open Trip?

Yes. Our standard shared-trip arrangement requires a minimum of 10 participants. Alternative vessel arrangements may be offered if the minimum is not reached.

6. How do I confirm my reservation?

Our team will provide the reservation form and applicable Terms & Conditions. Once you have reviewed and signed the documents, payment instructions will be issued. Your booking is confirmed after payment verification.

7. Can I change my travel date?

Date changes are subject to availability and the applicable supplier agreement. Please contact our team before making any changes to your reservation.

8. Can I request a private cabin on a shared trip?

Cabin requests are subject to vessel configuration and availability. Our team will check with the operator before confirming your request.
